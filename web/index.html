Powered by TfL Open Data. Trains don't report their position, so it's estimated from arrival predictions by the same Python code that drives the LED panel, running here in your browser via Pyodide. Tap a station to see its next trains. Each direction has its own track and trains keep left, as on real UK railways. White = train, orange = station, brown = track, red = delays (from TfL's line status, or a train whose arrival keeps slipping), grey = the line isn't running so the train probably isn't in service, blue = due at the station you tapped within 10 minutes. Stations without room for a label show their name when tapped.
